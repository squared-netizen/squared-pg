## Content

`lua/` contains the Lua implementation of generator functionality.

Lua modules MUST have clearly defined ownership and responsibilities. Modules
MUST NOT silently cross architectural boundaries by reaching into unrelated
subsystems through global state.

Lua-specific development conventions are defined by `lua/AGENTS.md`.
