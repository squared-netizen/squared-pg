## Learning design

Each chapter begins with measurable learning objectives and closes with key terms, a summary, review questions, and practice exercises. This follows the OpenStax pattern of objectives, concept summaries, vocabulary, review, and practice assessment. The first draft is intentionally concise; examples and explanations will expand through real projects.
